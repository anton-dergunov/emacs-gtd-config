#+TITLE: The Calendar
#+OPTIONS: toc:t
#+STARTUP: inlineimages

[[file:Index.org][↑ Back to the documentation index]]

Where the [[file:Agenda.org][Agenda]] answers /what should I focus on/, the *Calendar*
answers /what is happening over a stretch of time/. It is a pure date-and-time
view of your scheduled tasks and deadlines — a day, a week, a month, a year, or
any number of days you ask for — with no high-priority or in-progress sections
mixed in.

* Opening it
Press =C-c p c c= (Productivity → Plan & Review → Calendar → Open), or run =M-x
ps/show-calendar=. It opens at a single day. To open straight at a span:

| Key         | Opens          |
|-------------+----------------|
| =C-c p c c= | Calendar (day) |
| =C-c p c d= | a single day   |
| =C-c p c w= | a week         |
| =C-c p c m= | a month        |
| =C-c p c y= | a year         |

Once it is open you can change the span at any time without reopening — see
/Switching the span/ below. Opening it takes over whichever window you're in
(splitting first only if it's the only one open) — every other open window is
left exactly as it was.

You can also *click a date stamp* in any Org file (or press =C-c C-o= on it):
that opens the Calendar Day view for exactly that date — the same view as the
menu, with the span buttons. Clicking a date /range/ opens the Calendar spanning
those days.

* How it reads
The Calendar is a plain list — no sections, no timeline, no grid. *Day* shows a
header naming the day and, beneath it, the tasks dated on that day: timed tasks
first, sorted by time, with the time on the right (=08:00–08:30=); then the day's
untimed scheduled tasks and deadlines. Everything is in the normal text colour —
there is no overdue red, because…

…it shows only what is dated on the day you are looking at: nothing is carried
forward, warned about in advance, or shown as overdue, and no "x days ago"
labels. Overdue tasks live on the [[file:Agenda.org][Agenda]], by design — the Calendar answers
"what is dated for this day", not "what is outstanding".

*Week*, *Month* and *Year* put the whole span in the top header (e.g. =18–24 May
2026 · W21=, =May 2026=, =2026= — the week also shows its ISO week number) and
then list each day as its own *section* — a left-aligned, bold heading on a soft
grey band (=Monday · 18 May=) that you can collapse or expand. Days with nothing
on them are skipped, so only the days that matter appear. Weeks start on
whichever day your calendar is configured to start on, and a week/month/year
always covers the whole calendar period containing the date you were on.

Every line uses the same aligned columns, category icons, and pills as the rest
of the agenda, so the Calendar looks consistent — it is just simpler. The rich
timeline, the now-indicator, and the scheduled / due / overdue sections belong to
the [[file:Agenda.org][Agenda]]; the Calendar deliberately leaves them out. The mode line names the
current view and span, e.g. =Calendar · Week=, and that title doubles as a
*view switcher*: click it to jump straight to the [[file:Agenda.org][Agenda]],
[[file:Tasks-view.org][Tasks]], [[file:Planning-tools.org][Availability or
Conflicts]], or another Calendar span. Whichever you pick takes over the
window you're in, leaving any other open window untouched.

* Switching the span
The top header carries a small control strip of clickable buttons:

- *D W M Y* — switch to a day / week / month / year view in place. The current
  span is filled in.
- *Range…* — pick an arbitrary start and end date with Org's date picker (the
  same one =C-c C-s= uses, entered at the bottom of the frame); the Calendar then
  shows exactly that range. Only the dates matter — any time entered is ignored.
- the chevrons =‹= =›= step backward / forward by the current span (a week at a
  time in week view, a month at a time in month view, and so on); =⊙= jumps back
  to today and =⟳= reloads.

The keyboard equivalents Org provides also work: =v d=, =v w=, =v m=, =v y= to
switch span, and =b= / =f= to step back and forward.

In week/month/year, press =TAB= on a day section (or click it) to collapse or
expand that day.

* Under the hood / Customize
- The Calendar is the custom command =g=, defined alongside the Agenda in
  =config.org= → /Org Mode → Agenda → Super agenda/. It uses no super-agenda
  grouping and no time grid, and scopes every item to its own date (so nothing is
  carried forward or shown as overdue) — every span is Org's native per-day
  layout, kept as a plain list.
- Clicking a date stamp opens the Calendar because =config.org= points Org's
  timestamp-follow at the =g= command (=ps/calendar-follow-timestamp=), so a click
  and the menu give the identical view.
- The span-switch controls, the top control row, the per-day sections, and the
  time pills live in =lisp/ps-agenda-layout.el=; tunable faces are
  =ps/agenda-layout-day-section= (per-day grey band), =ps/agenda-layout-control-label=
  (span label), and =ps/agenda-layout-span-button= / =-span-button-active= (the
  =D W M Y= / =Range…= buttons).  The bindings are =ps/show-calendar= and
  =ps/show-calendar-{day,week,month,year}= under the =C-c p c= prefix (see
  [[file:Keybindings.org][Keybindings]]).
- Collapsing day sections reuses =lisp/ps-agenda-fold.el= (the same =TAB=/click
  folding as the Agenda's sections).
- The mode-line label (=Calendar · Day/Week/…=) and its click-to-switch popup
  are =ps/mode-line--agenda-render= / =ps/mode-line--agenda-click= in
  =lisp/ps-mode-line.el=.
- A long span (a year) only ever scans dated entries: the =g= command limits
  =org-agenda-entry-types= and turns off blocked-task dimming, and the conflict
  check is skipped for the Calendar — so it stays as quick as Org's per-day scan
  allows.
- The minute auto-refresh that moves the now-indicator runs only for the
  [[file:Agenda.org][Agenda]]; the Calendar (and the Tasks list) is never rebuilt behind your back,
  so it stays on whatever day you navigated to.
