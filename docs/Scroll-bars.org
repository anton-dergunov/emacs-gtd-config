#+TITLE: Scroll bars
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil
#+STARTUP: inlineimages

[[file:Index.org][↑ Back to the documentation index]]

This config replaces the native scroll bar with a custom one drawn at the right
edge of every window. It stays out of the way and matches your colour theme.

* What it does
- *Auto-hides.* The scrollbar is invisible until you need it. It appears when
  you scroll a window, then fades a moment after you stop — just like the overlay
  scroll bars in macOS, VS Code, or Obsidian. (Set =ps/scrollbar-show-on-hover=
  to also reveal it on mouse hover.)
- *Shows your place.* The thumb's height reflects how much of the buffer is on
  screen, and its position tracks where you are in the document.
- *Calm and theme-aware.* It is a soft, muted pill that takes its colour from
  your theme, so it never looks harsh — unlike the native macOS bar, which is
  always a hard, dark stripe that can't be recoloured or hidden.
- *Click and drag.* Press and drag the thumb with the mouse to scroll, the same
  way you would in any other app.
- *Everywhere.* It works in all windows — Org files, =*Messages*=, the Claude
  Code terminal, and any split.

Smooth, pixel-precise momentum scrolling with the trackpad or mouse wheel is
enabled alongside it.

* Tuning it
The thumb is on by default. A few things you can adjust (see below for where):
- how long it stays before fading,
- whether it also appears on mouse hover (not just on scroll),
- how wide the pill is.

* Under the hood / Customize
- Scrollbar: =lisp/ps-scrollbar.el=; settings live in =config.org= → /Settings →
  Scroll bars/ (=ps/scrollbar-hide-delay=, =ps/scrollbar-show-on-hover=,
  =ps/scrollbar-width=, =ps/scrollbar-min-thumb-pixels=). The colour comes
  from the =ps/scrollbar-thumb= face (and =ps/scrollbar-thumb-active= while
  hovering or dragging). Turn the whole thing off with =M-x ps/scrollbar-mode=.
- Smooth scrolling: =config.org= → /Editor & UI → Smooth scrolling (ultra-scroll)/.
