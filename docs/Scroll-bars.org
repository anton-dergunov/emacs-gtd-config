#+TITLE: Scroll bars
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil
#+STARTUP: inlineimages

[[file:Index.org][↑ Back to the documentation index]]

This config replaces the native scroll bar with a custom scroll-position
indicator at the right edge of every window. It stays out of the way and matches
your colour theme.

* What it does
- *Auto-hides.* The indicator is invisible until you need it. A small pill
  appears when you scroll a window, then gradually fades out a moment after you
  stop — like the overlay scroll bars in macOS, VS Code, or Obsidian. It also
  appears when you hover the mouse directly over its own track, so you can find
  it without scrolling first (set =ps/scrollbar-show-on-hover= to =nil= to turn
  that off).
- *Click to jump.* A single click anywhere in the scrollbar track (the thin
  fringe at the window's right edge) repositions the view so the thumb centres
  on the clicked point. Works whether you click on the pill itself or on the
  bare track above or below it.
- *Shows your place.* The pill's height reflects how much of the buffer is on
  screen, and its position tracks where you are in the document.
- *Calm and theme-aware.* It is a soft, muted pill that takes its colour from
  your theme, so it never looks harsh — unlike the native macOS bar, which is
  always a hard, dark stripe that can't be recoloured or hidden.
- *Everywhere.* It works in all windows — Org files, =*Messages*=, the Claude
  Code terminal, and any split.

Scroll with the trackpad, mouse wheel, or keyboard. Smooth, pixel-precise
momentum scrolling is enabled alongside it.

* Tuning it
The indicator is on by default. A few things you can adjust (see below for
where):
- how long it stays visible before fading ( =ps/scrollbar-hide-delay= ),
- how quickly it fades out ( =ps/scrollbar-fade-duration= , 0 for abrupt hide),
- whether it also appears on mouse hover (not just on scroll),
- how wide the pill is.

* Under the hood / Customize
- Indicator: =lisp/ps-scrollbar.el=; settings live in =config.org= → /Settings →
  Scroll bars/ ( =ps/scrollbar-hide-delay= , =ps/scrollbar-fade-duration= ,
  =ps/scrollbar-show-on-hover= , =ps/scrollbar-width= ,
  =ps/scrollbar-min-thumb-pixels= , =ps/scrollbar-click-to-scroll= ).
  The colour comes from the =ps/scrollbar-thumb= face.
  Turn it off with =M-x ps/scrollbar-mode=.
- Smooth scrolling: =config.org= → /Editor & UI → Smooth scrolling (ultra-scroll)/.
