#+TITLE: Planning tools
#+OPTIONS: toc:t

Three focused tools help you plan around your scheduled time: find free slots,
spot conflicts, and move events between timezones. Each opens its own buffer
with on-screen controls, so you can adjust options without remembering any
syntax.

* Availability — find free slots
Computes your open time over the coming days from the scheduled events in your
Org files, so you can answer "when am I free?" at a glance.

- Open with =C-c p v= (Productivity → Plan & Review → Availability), or
  =M-x ps/org-show-availability=. It takes over whichever window you're in
  (splitting first only if it's the only one open) and is reachable from the
  mode-line view switcher (click the title in Agenda/Calendar/Tasks/Conflicts).
- The buffer shows free slots per day; use its controls to change the window of
  days, working hours, and formatting.

Defaults you can change (see below): how many days to look ahead, the start/end
of your working day, a buffer between meetings, the minimum slot length, whether
to include weekends, 12h vs 24h time, and the layout.

* Conflicts — spot overlaps and tight gaps
Scans your scheduled events and reports two kinds of trouble: events that
*overlap*, and gaps between back-to-back events that are uncomfortably *small*.

- Open with =C-c p x= (Productivity → Plan & Review → Conflicts), or
  =M-x ps/show-conflicts=. Like Availability, it takes over whichever window
  you're in and is reachable from the mode-line view switcher.
- A lightweight version of this check also runs automatically in the
  [[file:Agenda.org][Agenda]], flagging overlapping timed events with =⚠
  overlap= and showing a total count next to the mode-line title — click it to
  open this view.

Defaults you can change: the minimum acceptable gap between events, and whether
to include past events.

* Timezone shift — move events between zones
Shifts the timestamps in an Org file from one timezone to another and shows you
a preview before anything is written — useful when you've planned in one zone
and are travelling to another.

- Open with =C-c p t= (Productivity → Edit Org → Shift Timezones…), or
  =M-x ps/shift-timezone=.
- Review the preview, then confirm to apply.

* Under the hood / Customize
- *Availability:* =lisp/ps-availability.el=; defaults in =config.org= →
  /Availability/ (=ps/org-avail-*=: =days=, =work-start=, =work-end=,
  =buffer-minutes=, =min-duration=, =include-weekends=, =time-format=, =layout=).
- *Conflicts:* =lisp/ps-conflicts.el=; defaults in =config.org= → /Conflicts/
  (=ps/conflicts-gap-minutes=, =ps/conflicts-include-past=).
- *Timezone:* =lisp/ps-timezone.el=.
